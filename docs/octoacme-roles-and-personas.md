# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Technical Lead / Engineering Lead

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide architectural guidance, ensure code quality standards, and help the team navigate complex technical decisions. They serve as the bridge between the engineering team and product/project leadership, translating technical constraints into business impact.

### Responsibilities
- Define and maintain technical architecture and design patterns
- Review design decisions and technical approaches for feasibility and scalability
- Manage technical debt and prioritize refactoring efforts
- Conduct code reviews and enforce quality standards
- Identify and mitigate technical risks
- Mentor junior developers and guide skill development
- Participate in sprint planning to assess technical estimates

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, maintainable, and performant code
- Reduce technical debt and improve codebase health
- Enable team velocity through clear architectural guidance
- Ensure security and operational best practices

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **With Developers**: Provides architectural guidance, code review feedback, and mentorship
- **With Product Manager**: Discusses feasibility of features, technical constraints, and trade-offs
- **With Project Manager**: Flags technical risks and dependencies
- **With QA/Testing**: Collaborates on test strategy and quality gates
- **With Security Lead**: Ensures security requirements are integrated into architecture

### Typical Communication
- Technical design discussions and architecture reviews
- Code review comments and technical guidance
- Sprint planning sessions to assess technical feasibility
- Risk identification and mitigation planning

---

## UX/Design Lead

### Role Summary
UX/Design Leads define the user experience strategy, conduct user research, and validate design decisions. They advocate for the customer throughout the project, ensuring solutions are usable, accessible, and delightful.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and gather customer insights
- Define user experience strategy and design system
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and high-fidelity designs
- Validate design decisions with users and stakeholders
- Define accessibility and usability standards
- Collaborate with developers on implementation consistency
- Iterate on designs based on user feedback

### Goals
- Deliver user-centric solutions that meet customer needs
- Maintain design consistency across products
- Improve user satisfaction and adoption
- Reduce support burden through intuitive design

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **With Product Manager**: Collaborates on defining requirements and user needs
- **With Developers**: Works closely on design-to-development handoff
- **With QA/Testing**: Partners on usability testing and accessibility validation
- **With Project Manager**: Coordinates design milestones and dependencies
- **With Stakeholders**: Presents design decisions and gathers feedback

### Typical Communication
- Design review meetings and critique sessions
- User research findings and insights sharing
- Design specifications and component documentation
- Usability testing and feedback incorporation

---

## Security Lead

### Role Summary
Security Leads ensure that security and compliance requirements are integrated throughout the project lifecycle. They identify security risks, define controls, and help teams build secure by default.

### Responsibilities
- Identify security and compliance requirements early
- Conduct security threat modeling and risk assessments
- Review architecture and code for security vulnerabilities
- Define and enforce security standards and best practices
- Manage security testing and vulnerability remediation
- Ensure compliance with relevant regulations and standards
- Provide security training and guidance to the team

### Goals
- Minimize security vulnerabilities and compliance risk
- Build security into the product from inception
- Enable the team to make informed security decisions
- Maintain customer trust through secure practices

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **With Technical Lead**: Partners on architecture and design security
- **With Developers**: Reviews code for vulnerabilities and provides guidance
- **With QA/Testing**: Coordinates security testing and pen testing
- **With Release Manager**: Ensures security sign-off before release
- **With Project Manager**: Escalates security blockers and dependencies

### Typical Communication
- Security threat modeling sessions
- Code review and architecture security reviews
- Security testing coordination
- Incident response and vulnerability management

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate release planning, deployment workflows, and post-release verification. They ensure releases are predictable, well-communicated, and low-risk.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain release plans and schedules
- Coordinate dependencies across teams
- Prepare release documentation and release notes
- Manage deployment workflows and checklists
- Execute deployments and monitor for issues
- Coordinate rollback procedures if needed
- Gather post-release metrics and feedback

### Goals
- Execute predictable, low-risk releases
- Minimize deployment-related incidents
- Ensure clear communication throughout release cycles
- Enable rapid, reliable deployments

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **With Project Manager**: Coordinates release schedule and milestones
- **With Developers**: Manages code freeze and deployment coordination
- **With QA/Testing**: Coordinates pre-release testing and smoke tests
- **With Technical Lead**: Partners on release architecture and rollback planning
- **With Stakeholders**: Communicates release status and timing

### Typical Communication
- Release planning and status meetings
- Deployment readiness reviews
- Release notes and stakeholder communication
- Post-release incident response

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters and Agile Coaches facilitate agile ceremonies, remove blockers, and help teams improve their delivery practices. They serve as coaches rather than managers, helping teams embrace agile principles.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, daily standups, and retrospectives
- Remove impediments and escalate blockers
- Coach team on agile practices and ceremonies
- Track sprint metrics and team velocity
- Help teams continuously improve their processes
- Facilitate communication and collaboration
- Manage sprint board and backlog organization

### Goals
- Enable high-performing, self-organizing teams
- Improve delivery predictability and velocity
- Create a culture of continuous improvement
- Remove obstacles to team productivity

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **With Project Manager**: Coordinates planning and risk escalation
- **With All Team Members**: Facilitates ceremonies and removes blockers
- **With Product Manager**: Assists with backlog refinement and prioritization
- **With Team Lead**: Partners on team health and process improvement

### Typical Communication
- Sprint ceremonies and team coaching
- Retrospective facilitation and action item tracking
- Blocker escalation and impediment removal
- Metrics reporting and process improvement discussions
